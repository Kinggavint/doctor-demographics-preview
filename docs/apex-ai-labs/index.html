Apex AI Labs: marketing guided by Doctor Demographics research

Apex AI Labs harnesses the power of Doctor Demographics. It uses demographic, deep demographic and psychographic research to direct its SEO and AEO (AI answer) work for healthcare practices.

That means Apex AI Labs starts with an unusual level of detail about each client, their patients and their potential patients.

Visit Apex AI Labs (opens in a new tab)

Three layers of research

Demographic research

Who lives around a practice: the population, ages, incomes and households, and how the area is changing.

Deep demographic research

A closer look at the neighborhoods a practice draws from, the competition nearby and how far patients travel.

Psychographic research

What local patients value, how they choose a provider and what they look for when they need care.

How the research guides the work

Apex AI Labs builds online visibility for healthcare practices. The research decides where that effort goes.

  • AI-powered websites

    Websites built around the patients a practice wants to reach, with pages, wording and services shaped by who lives and searches nearby.

  • SEO

    Keywords, location pages and local listings chosen from the research, so search work focuses on the people and places most likely to become patients.

  • AI answer visibility (AEO)

    Clear, well organized answers to the questions local patients ask, so AI assistants and answer engines can find, understand and mention the practice.

How it pairs with Doctor Demographics reports

The reports and the marketing work from the same research, in three short steps.

  1. Start with the research

    A Doctor Demographics report maps the area around the practice: who lives there, how it is changing and where the opportunity is.

  2. Turn it into a plan

    Apex AI Labs uses the demographic, deep demographic and psychographic findings to choose which patients, services and areas to focus on.

  3. Build and refine

    Apex AI Labs builds the website, SEO and AI answer work around that plan, then checks it against the same research as the practice grows.
