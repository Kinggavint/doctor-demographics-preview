Telephone Consultation Description

About Telephone Consultations

Help when you need it. The Telephone Consultation is used for doctors in the following situations:

  • Marketing questions (which media, budgets, etc)
  • Broad areas that you are evaluating
  • Multiple sites that you are trying to choose between

Spouses, consultants, and even parents are welcome on the calls. Put as much in writing for us BEFORE the consultation so you won’t have to use the time giving explanations or “set-up.”

Keep notes during the call as numbers will come thick and fast. We do original research on the sites. This is not “shooting from the hip.” Rather, it is the result of the analysis.

Be specific in what you want to know BEFORE the consultation so we can be specific.

Note:

  • Units of 30 minutes are standard. Once an appointment has been set, if you are late, we will use the balance of the time rather than starting an additional 30 minutes.
  • No refund for missed appointments. However, if WE miss for some reason, you get an hour when we reschedule or refund (your choice).
  • We do not do Telephone Consultations without an appointment. We just cannot have you call and chat then expect us to have good data and recommendations. All this means that there are a limited number of appointment times per week.
  • We do not track insurance offered or accepted in the region.
