Which practice types do you write reports for?

Doctor Demographics writes hand-written site, market, competition and marketing reports for private practices. Each report includes a 20-minute follow-up call. When we email the finished report, we include a booking link; you can also reply to that email to set a time. Spouses, partners and consultants are welcome on the call.

The pages below are for the specialties in Wave 1, plus the existing dental page. Later pages will follow for other specialties Doctor Demographics has confirmed they serve, once the location factors and FAQ answers for those pages are in hand.

Practice types with dedicated guides

  • Dental Demographic Reports — for general dentists and dental specialists.
  • Veterinary Demographic Reports — households and growth, pet ownership and spending, income, nearby clinics, and visibility, parking and access.
  • Optometry Demographic Reports — residents and daytime workers, age mix, income and education, people per optometrist including retail optical chains, and a visible retail site.
  • Orthodontic Demographic Reports — children ages 7 to 17 within a 10 to 15 minute drive, young families and new housing, income, referring general dentists, orthodontists per child, and nearby schools.
