State Briefing Description
State Briefings give doctor's a little guidance on which States hold most promise and what should doctors know about these states to make a decisions.
Data on every County in the State is included on a spreadsheet with key demographics. Competition per County data are also included. Frank discussion on the merits of liabilities on each large County and major City is included.
Formats vary State-to-State to reflect size and demographic peculiarities.
