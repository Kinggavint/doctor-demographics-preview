Dental Demographics
All the Resources Dentists Need
For over 30 Years, Doctor Demographics has supported Dentists in their search for the perfect place to put a practice with Dental Demographics.
Our founder, Scott McDonald pioneered the dental demographics industry after his time at the California Dental Association as a young man serving as their marketing director.
Providing Dental Demographics for Practice Owners (General & Speciality)
From our Best Sites Report to our Site Specific Reports, we have generated 10,000+ reports, consultations, and mentorship to dentists in every state. This includes both general and speciality doctors.
Our Two Most Popular Demographic Reports for Dentists
Best Sites Report
This service combines a report and consultation, analyzing user-defined areas (up to 5 counties) to identify the 5 best sites, and the reasons why we feel that way. This report includes a 20-minute consultation.
Learn More
Site Viability Report
This dental demographic report evaluates practice location viability using data from 14 zip codes and relevant market drive-time radii. It includes competition analysis, expert site specific recommendations, and a 20-minute consultation.
